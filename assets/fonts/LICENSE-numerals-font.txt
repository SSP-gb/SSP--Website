The files ssp-numerals-regular.woff and ssp-numerals-bold.woff are subsets (digits and
a few symbols only) of the font "Carlito", renamed "SSP Numerals" as the licence requires
for modified versions. They are used on this website so that numbers appear in a
Calibri-style face on every device.
