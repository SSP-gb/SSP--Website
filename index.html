About Us

Saltwater Strategy & Projects brings together business strategy and project management. What connects them is succession: moving well from one stage to the next. That might be a project reaching completion, a team member stepping up, a business growing, or an owner preparing to hand over or sell. Each one needs a clear plan, the right people and someone who keeps it on track.

That work taught her that business decisions are rarely just business decisions. They shape families, retirements and the people who work in the business.

FAQs

What outcomes can business owners expect?

A clear picture of where you want to go, and a practical, step-by-step plan to get there that you can start on straight away or when the timing suits you.

What do you mean by succession?

Succession is any move from one stage to the next: an owner stepping back or selling, a team member stepping up, a practice developing its next generation of advisers, or a project moving from plan to completion. Each one goes better with a clear plan, the right people and someone keeping it on track. That is where strategy and project management meet.

Why are business owners talking about 30 June 2027?

Changes to capital gains tax (CGT) are now law and start on 1 July 2027. The 50% CGT discount is being replaced by indexation, with a 30% minimum tax on capital gains made from that date. Gains built up before 1 July 2027 keep the old rules, so the value of your business at that date helps set how a future gain is taxed, even if you don’t sell for years.

Owners can generally use a market valuation or a government formula to set that value. Knowing what your business is worth now, and where value can be built before then, puts you in a stronger position either way. A well-supported value is also useful when you need to show a bank what your business is worth. Separate changes to how discretionary trusts are taxed are planned from 1 July 2028.

This is general information only. Please talk to your accountant or registered tax agent about how the changes apply to you.

What kinds of business valuation do you offer?

Snapshot valuation: a quicker estimate for internal planning. It shows roughly where your business stands today and where value could be built.

Full valuation: a more detailed valuation prepared in line with the ATO’s market valuation guidelines. It takes more time and documentation, and suits tax, transfer and lending purposes.

A valuation depends on the information provided and is not a guarantee of a sale price.

Do you give financial, tax or legal advice?

No. Our background includes financial planning, but we do not provide financial, tax or legal advice. We work alongside your financial adviser, accountant and lawyer, and can bring them into the plan.

Do you work with professional practices?

Yes. We work with financial planning, accounting and legal practices on succession, leadership and team development, including workshops and coaching for principals and senior leaders and programs to develop next-generation and Professional Year advisers.

What outcomes can clients expect for their projects?

Every part of the project considered, actioned and monitored in line with Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK) principles.

Do you provide design services?

We don’t take on design work ourselves. With many years of civil infrastructure design experience behind us, we critically review designs and give clear feedback to clients and designers to improve the project.

Projects: private developers, businesses, contractors and consultants, mining companies, and federal, state and local government.
